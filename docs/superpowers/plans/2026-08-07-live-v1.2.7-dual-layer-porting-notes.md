# Live 1.2.7-1.2.9 changes to carry into the Dual-Layer Archive version

The Dual-Layer Archive checkout was not modified by this live-plugin release.

## Required parity changes

1. Replace employee-record construction inside `can_view_user()` with direct primary-group lookup to prevent delegated-permission recursion.
2. Treat `compliance_lead` as full-access inside the compliance dashboard only: unrestricted dashboard scope, active/inactive employees, records, reviews, reminders, announcements, subscriber employee management, and packet generation. Do not grant WordPress administrator capabilities.
3. Support authorized cross-user LearnDash certificate acquisition without administrator impersonation. The live version uses a one-time internal broker bound to the packet job, target employee, course, certificate index, and job lifetime.
4. Preserve same-origin requests, TLS verification, redirect blocking, private packet storage, PDF byte/page limits, target-scope rechecks, and fail-closed no-partial-packet behavior.
5. Limit incomplete orientation requirements to LearnDash courses assigned to the employee; completed mapped courses remain valid evidence even if later unenrolled.
6. Route Certificate Builder posts through `LearnDash_Certificate_Builder\Component\PDF` before the legacy TCPDF fallback so delegated-role packets retain backgrounds and dynamic employee/course fields.
7. Keep the combined Included Course Details table, then add one full-description page for each completed course and place its certificate immediately after that page. The dedicated page shows course name, unshortened LearnDash short description, completion date, mapped credit hours, and training category.
8. Move Employee into the bordered cover details box above Role, Date of Hire, First Service Date, and Worked Alone Date. Display employee-anniversary mode as `Annual Cycle Rule: Employee Hire Date`.

## Archive-specific integration note

Do not copy the live broker directly into the immutable archive write path. Adapt certificate acquisition through the approved Dual-Layer Archive certificate-acquisition coordinator so provenance, byte validation, persistence, and archive-unit-of-work rules remain intact.

## Regression checks

- Compliance Lead sees all compliance-dashboard elements but cannot manage privileged WordPress accounts or site administration.
- HR Manager with delegated Edit Training Records access renders the dashboard without recursion and remains group-scoped.
- Administrator, Compliance Lead, HR Manager, and Group Leader packet generation succeeds for authorized employees.
- Out-of-scope targets, replayed broker tokens, non-LearnDash sources, invalid certificate output, and partial packets remain rejected.
- Unassigned orientation mappings do not fail the 30-day result, while incomplete assigned orientation courses still do.
- Certificate Builder packets contain their background and dynamic employee/course fields for Administrator, Compliance Lead, and HR Manager.
- Course pages and matching certificates alternate in the final packet without removing or changing the combined course table.
- Full LearnDash short descriptions are not truncated on dedicated course pages, and the cover details box/annual-cycle label match the live layout.

## Future Clinical Precision drawer parity — documentation only

The live 1.7.0 employee drawer now follows the corrected Stitch Clinical Precision handoff: fixed identity and tab regions, independently scrollable tab content, fixed actions, compact training rows and matching Documents, Communications and Administration cards. This note does not authorize or implement a Dual-Layer runtime change.

Any future port must preserve the archive checkout's approved coordinators, provenance and immutable-write rules; reuse its own permission/action entry points; and omit unsupported prototype upload/request controls, record hashes, invented identifiers, Tailwind and external fonts. Port acceptance must separately verify role scope, keyboard tabs, scroll containment, announcement isolation, evidence access and packet actions.

The accepted live parity target also includes dashboard-font inheritance, underline-only tab selection, compact borderless record cards, method/date/time-first communication entries, a real-data employment/System Meta block, and a two-action footer with Compliance Report retained inside More. These are documentation-only port requirements and do not authorize changes to the archive checkout.
